GAS — DO NOT / MUST
  • Do not run gas in air ducts, chimneys/vents, shafts, solid walls, partitions, or concrete slabs.
  • Do not use gas piping as a grounding electrode. Bond electrically continuous per NFPA 70.
  • Must: 2-elbow swing on branches from risers · grade 2% down to drips · insulating coupling at underground-to-aboveground transition.
  • Color code (meter room): 15–5 psi = brown · reduced pressure = yellow. Door sign: “Gas Meter Room — No Storage Permitted” (1" letters).
